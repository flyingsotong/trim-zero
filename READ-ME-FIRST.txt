Trim Zero v0.1.0
===============

Digital trim for an analogue wheel.

1. Install the .NET 8 Desktop Runtime if you do not have it:
   https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/8.0

2. In MSFS Controls, clear the binding on your wheel's own "Elevator Trim Axis"
   action. If the sim still binds the axis, the sim and Trim Zero will fight.

3. Run TrimZero.exe.

4. Load a flight. In settings, roll to full nose-down and press SET; roll to full
   nose-up and press SET. This measures the wheel's real stops.

5. Set the aircraft to takeoff trim, park the wheel on the TAKE OFF mark, press ZERO.

6. Fly. ZERO re-datums at any time. Drift shows when the aircraft has moved away from
   the mark.

No network, no telemetry, no updater. Settings live in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\TrimZero\settings.json.
